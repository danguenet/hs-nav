# Store asset provenance

- Product screenshots were rendered from the local `preview/popup.html` and built `dist/options.html` pages.
- Promo tiles use the repository's HS Nav icon, the captured Search UI, and product copy derived from the README.
- Exports were rendered at their final canvas sizes and verified as RGB PNG files without alpha channels.
- The demo video was recorded from the local preview and settings pages, then exported as a 1920×1080 H.264 MP4 with no third-party media.
