# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

HS Nav is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Contributors should:

- communicate constructively and assume good intent;
- welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- focus feedback on ideas and code rather than people; and
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's confidential information.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, and other project spaces, as well as when someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [dan@performancerevops.com](mailto:dan@performancerevops.com). Include relevant links or context. Reports will be reviewed in confidence and handled proportionately, which may include a warning, removal of content, or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
